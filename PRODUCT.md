# Tania's Executive Assistant Portfolio

## Users
- **Primary**: Potential clients seeking Executive Assistant / Operations Support services (executives, small business owners, startups)
- **Secondary**: Recruiters, agencies, referral partners
- **Goal**: Convert visitors into inquiries for VA services or contract work

## Product Purpose
A professional, high-trust portfolio website showcasing Tania's executive assistant expertise, operational skills, and proven ability to manage complex administrative workflows remotely.

## Brand Tone
- Professional and trustworthy
- Competent, detail-oriented, reliable
- Warm but formal
- Solution-focused, action-oriented
- Zero fluff—every element earns its place

## Anti-References
- ❌ Overly trendy or playful (not a creative agency)
- ❌ Generic "consultant" aesthetic (navy + gold clichés)
- ❌ Busy/cluttered layouts (contradicts "organized operations" message)
- ❌ Heavy use of stock photography
- ❌ Animated hero sections or excess motion
- ❌ "Guru" or overpromising copy

## Strategic Principles
1. **Trust through clarity** - Simple, well-organized information architecture
2. **Competence through specificity** - Real tools, real experience, real results
3. **Accessibility** - Works perfectly for executives reviewing on their phone or desktop
4. **Conversion focused** - Easy paths to contact/inquiry

## Register
Brand (portfolio IS the product; design communicates professional identity)

## Key Messages
- "I handle the complexity so you can focus on growth"
- Deep expertise across platforms (Kajabi, Xero, HubSpot, WordPress, Squarespace, etc.)
- 5+ years supporting executives across multiple industries
- Remote-first, reliable, discreet

## Color Strategy
**Warm, earthy, premium aesthetic**. Cream + beige as primary palette (not cold blues). Warm gold accents for CTAs and emphasis. Supports both light and dark modes seamlessly—cream dominates light mode, warm dark browns dominate dark mode. This creates a sophisticated, trust-building visual that stands out from generic SaaS blue.

## Current State
- Clean, functional Next.js portfolio
- Solid information architecture
- Professional but safe design
- Room for: better typography, refined spacing, more visual hierarchy, strategic color depth
